# Learning Process

1. **What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

   It is a learning method where you explain a topic in plain, simple language, as if teaching a beginner, to expose gaps in your understanding.

---

2. **In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?**

   The most interesting idea for me was the 20-hour rule, which shows that you can become reasonably good at a new skill with focused practice instead of years of effort. I also found the Pomodoro Technique useful for staying focused and beating procrastination.

---

3. **What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

   - **Active Mode:** Deliberate, concentrated thinking used for problem solving and working with ideas you already know.
   - **Diffuse Mode:** Relaxed, background thinking that helps you make new connections and see the bigger picture when learning something unfamiliar.

---

4. **According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?**

   - Break the skill down into smaller sub-skills.
   - Learn just enough to practice and correct your own mistakes.
   - Eliminate distractions and other barriers to practice.
   - Commit to at least 20 hours of deliberate practice.

---

5. **What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?**

   - I will study in distraction-free sessions using the Pomodoro Technique.
   - I will explain what I learn in simple words, following the Feynman Technique, to check my understanding.
   - I will take short breaks or walks so my mind can switch to diffuse mode and absorb new ideas.
   - I will break big topics into small parts and practice each one regularly until I am comfortable with it.
