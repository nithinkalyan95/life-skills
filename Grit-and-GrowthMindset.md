# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**

Grit is the passion and persistence to keep chasing a long-term goal. It means showing up day after day, for years rather than weeks, and treating life like a marathon instead of a sprint.

## Question 2
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**

The video describes two ways of thinking about ability. People with a **growth mindset** believe skills can be built through effort, so they trust they can keep learning and improving. People with a **fixed mindset** believe skills are something you're born with, so they feel there's little point in trying to change.

## Question 3
**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

**Internal Locus of Control** is the belief that you are in charge of what happens in your life, and that your own effort shapes your success.

**Key points from the video:**
* **Your effort counts.** When you see that your work changes your results, it's easier to stay motivated.
* **Don't hand your power to excuses.** Blaming bad luck or telling yourself "I'm just not smart enough" is a quick way to give up.
* **Start small.** Solve a small problem in your life, then remind yourself, "I did that through my own hard work."
