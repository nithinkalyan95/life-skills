# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any **unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature**, whether physical, verbal, or non-verbal.

- Unwanted physical contact, touching, or inappropriate advances.
- Making sexually suggestive comments, jokes, or remarks.
- Sending inappropriate messages, emails, images, or videos.
- Displaying pornographic or sexually explicit content.
- Making someone uncomfortable through inappropriate gestures, staring, or personal comments.
- Threatening or offering workplace benefits in exchange for sexual favours.

## 2. What would you do if you faced or witnessed an incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

- I would document the incidents and preserve any relevant evidence, such as messages, emails, or screenshots.
- I would report the incident to the company's HR team, or the designated POSH representative.
- If I witnessed such behaviour, I would support the affected person and encourage them to report it.
- I would cooperate with the investigation while maintaining confidentiality.
- I would not ignore or encourage such behaviour, and I would ensure that the affected person is treated with respect.
