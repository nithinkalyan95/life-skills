# Listening and Assertive Communication

## **Question 1**
**What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?**

- Give your full attention and avoid distractions.
- Let the speaker finish before replying.
- Use short prompts like "I see, ok, go ahead" to show interest.
- Maintain eye contact and take notes if needed.
- Summarize what I heard to confirm understanding.

---

## **Question 2**
**According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)**

- Listen closely to the speaker.
- Repeat their message in your own words.
- Check that you understood it correctly.

---

## **Question 3**
**What are the obstacles in your listening process?**

- Small distractions break my focus.
- Background noise makes it hard to hear.

---

## **Question 4**
**What can you do to improve your listening?**

- Stay on the topic of the conversation.
- Silence my phone before important discussions.
- Choose a quiet place when possible.

---

## **Question 5**
**When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?**

- When I want to avoid an argument.
- When I talk to seniors or people in authority.

---

## **Question 6**
**When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?**

I become forceful in urgent or chaotic situations where quick action is needed.

---

## **Question 7**
**When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day-to-day life?**

I go silent or give short replies when someone keeps disturbing me and I want to avoid a direct confrontation.

---

## **Question 8**
**How can you make your communication assertive? You can watch and analyse the videos, then think what would be a few steps you can apply in your own life?**

- Name my feelings and needs clearly.
- Start practicing in low-stakes situations.
- Keep a calm voice and steady posture.
- Use "I" statements instead of blaming.
- Keep my opinion brief, without over-explaining.
